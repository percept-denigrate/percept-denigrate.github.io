The Big five is a model for personality. It consists of 5 scores representing different aspects of personality:
- Extroversion: tendency to be outgoing and to seek high-stimulation environments
- Neuroticism: tendency for stress and negative feelings
- Agreableness: tendency to act kindly and avoid conflict
- Conscientiousness: discipline, capacity to plan and organize tasks
- Openness: tendency to seek new experiences

This model is preferred over many others models such as MBTI, as it is stable throughout time and predictive of life outcomes. In particular, conscientiousness is predictive of life and professional success, agreableness and low neuroticism are predictive of relationship stability.
