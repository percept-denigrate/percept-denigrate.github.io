Zero-knowledge [[Mathematical proofs|proofs]] (ZKP) are cryptographic algorithms to prove a problem is solved, without exchange of knowledge on the solution.

One approach is ZK-SNARK (zero-knowledge succint non-interactive arguments of knowledge):

- Succint: proofs are short
- Non-interactive: static proofs consisting of a single message from the prover
- Of knowledge: proof not only that the statement is true, but also that the prover knows a witness of the statement's veracity
